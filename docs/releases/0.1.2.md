# Syclop 0.1.2

- Smoother sidebar opening and closing, coordinated labels, and calmer remote-focus feedback.
- Short directional Settings transitions across Classic, Zen, and Horizon, including nested pages and Back navigation.
- Update offers show the installed and available versions, a patch-notes preview, and scrollable full release notes. Later stays selected initially; downloads require choosing Update now.
- Express source searches can use verified original and ASCII title spellings alongside the catalog title, including Aşk-ı Memnu / Ask-i Memnu. A bounded TVmaze lookup supports series without a TMDB key; configured TMDB metadata also supports original movie titles.
- Clear conflicting title-prefix years help exclude older remakes while preserving the exact catalog identity and season/episode numbers.

The update-offer improvements take effect after installing 0.1.2. Existing 0.1.1 installations retain their current dialog.

Source availability still depends on the configured provider. This update does not map original long episodes to shorter international edits or guarantee regional availability or subtitles.
