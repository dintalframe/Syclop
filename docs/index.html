Get started

Three steps to your next watch.

Requires Android 7.0+ and a media source you are authorized to use. Syclop does not supply media; service accounts and subscriptions are separate.

  1. Install Syclop.

    Download the signed APK from the latest release, install it on your TV, and complete first-run setup.

  2. Connect your source.

    Open Settings → Connected Services → Add-ons and media servers. Debrid services are in the same Connected Services menu.

  3. Make yourself at home.

    Choose a profile and start watching. Optional trackers under Connected accounts can sync supported library and watch-progress data.
